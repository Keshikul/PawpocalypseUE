Bestiary

Every enemy and boss in Pawpocalypse, redrawn in Sticker Box: one ink line, a white die-cut rim, two flat fills, toy-box colours. Each biome is shown on its own ground at game proportions, with the cat at that biome's stage for scale.

Biome by biome

Each enemy fills a square of its sprite_target_px from Data/EnemyData.csv, so sizes match the game. The cat is drawn at an approximate size.

The gristle, tier by tier

One brute in every biome. It walks on its knuckles and heads for the cat. The spikes, lumps and scars grow with the biome, so a deep gristle looks it.

Bosses

Bosses are drawn at 230 px, three to four times an enemy, so they carry more detail. They are the only enemies that are not scared: brows down, sure of themselves. Each one is shown with an enemy from its biome for scale.

What changed, and what is next

  • All 39 enemy drawings and 6 boss drawings are new, in one style. Each keeps the species cue the current art has: the vole has no ears, the pigeon has no neck, the seagull's wings are out, the ant has three segments, the hamster's cheeks are stuffed.
  • New jokes that read at 64 px: the factory rat has goggles, the add wears a plus because it heals the boss.
  • Fliers, golden mice and gristles keep one shared drawing each, as in the data. The bat replaces the flying mouse from City Rooftops on.
  • Next: every UI screen in this style, as concepts, before anything changes in Unreal.
