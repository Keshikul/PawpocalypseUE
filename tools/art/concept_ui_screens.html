UI Screens

Every screen in Pawpocalypse, redrawn in Sticker Box. The words are the game's approved UI text (ST_UI), the keys are the real bindings (Data/InputMap.csv), and the layout follows the handoff: the field fills the window, the menu dock sits on the right, the status reads top left.

Card stock and one ink. Every panel is cream card with a 3.5 px ink edge and a hard shadow. Rows inside are a darker cream with a thinner edge.

The brightest thing is what you can do. A yellow button means you can do it now. A dimmed one with a faded icon means you cannot yet.

Focus is a blue ring. Never a fill, so it reads from the couch and for colour-blind players. Every screen shows where focus starts.
